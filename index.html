About

I'm drawn to user-centered design and service innovation: projects that pair research rigor with a willingness to imagine differently. My work spans solo and collaborative projects, shaped by the needs, constraints, and research I encounter on each one. Outside of client work, I'm personally drawn to utopian and dystopian thinking, which keeps me curious about the assumptions behind the briefs I take on.

  • Skilled in UX, interaction design, and creative research
  • Experienced in both team-based and independent projects
  • Driven by curiosity, iteration, and purposeful design

This portfolio is a selection of that work. If something resonates, I'd love to connect.

On the research side, my MSc thesis, a qualitative study on AI and academic integrity in Swedish universities, involved 13 semi-structured interviews and thematic analysis.
